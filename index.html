05 • FROM LAB BENCH TO UNIVERSE

Real-World Optical Wonders

Click "Load in Big Light Lab" on any device to simulate its exact optical geometry!

🔭 CONCAVE MIRROR

Deep-Space Reflecting Telescopes

Giant segmented concave mirrors (like the James Webb Space Telescope) gather parallel starlight from billions of light-years away and concentrate every photon onto a tiny focal detector.

🚗 CONVEX MIRROR

Wide-Angle Side & Blind-Spot Mirrors

Outward-curving convex mirrors diverge incoming rays, packing three lanes of highway traffic into an upright, diminished virtual image—why objects appear closer than they are!

🔬 CONVEX LENS

Magnifying Loupes & Camera Objectives

Place a specimen inside the focal length of a biconvex lens to view a towering upright virtual image, or place it beyond F to project a crisp inverted image onto a camera sensor.

👓 CONCAVE LENS

Myopia Eyeglasses & Door Peepholes

Nearsighted eyes focus light slightly ahead of the retina. A diverging concave lens spreads the rays just enough so they land razor-sharp on the retina—and lets security peepholes show a whole hallway!
